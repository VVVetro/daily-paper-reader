## 摘要
理解城市医疗流动性对于医疗资源分配、医院容量规划和韧性城市治理至关重要。现有的医疗起点-终点（OD）预测方法主要依赖监督式深度学习，需要昂贵的模型训练，且对医院选择的行为机制解释性有限。本文提出了一种基于知识驱动的行为推理的免训练城市医疗OD预测框架，该框架利用结构化城市知识上的协作LLM推理取代基于梯度的神经网络学习。该框架将异构城市信息（包括医疗资源、人口特征、交通可达性、天气条件和历史流动性）组织成统一的知识图谱，通过多智能体推理流程实现证据检索、城市上下文理解、行为推理、偏好排序和贝叶斯验证。在包含137家医院和6341个预测任务的真实深圳数据集上的实验表明，该框架实现了CPC=0.5449、Top-1=66.19%、Top-5=68.03%、Top-10=74.03%，在所有Top-K指标上持续优于传统深度学习基线（例如，Top-5比MLP提升22.1%）。与使用相同骨干网络但无结构化推理的直接LLM相比，该框架将CPC提升了87.5%，Top-1准确率提升了141%，表明知识驱动的行为推理（而非原始LLM能力）是实现准确、可解释且无需训练的医疗OD预测的关键。这些发现凸显了城市智能将医疗流动性建模从数据拟合转向知识驱动的行为推理的潜力。

## Abstract
Understanding urban healthcare mobility is essential for healthcare resource allocation, hospital capacity planning, and resilient urban governance. Existing healthcare origin-destination (OD) prediction methods primarily rely on supervised deep learning, requiring expensive model training while providing limited interpretability into the behavioral mechanisms of hospital choice. This paper presents a training-free urban healthcare OD prediction framework based on knowledge-grounded behavioral reasoning, replacing gradient-based neural network learning with collaborative LLM reasoning over structured urban knowledge. The framework organizes heterogeneous urban information, including healthcare resources, population characteristics, transportation accessibility, weather conditions, and historical mobility, into a unified knowledge graph, enabling evidence retrieval, urban context understanding, behavioral reasoning, preference ranking, and Bayesian verification through a multi-agent reasoning pipeline. Experiments on a real-world Shenzhen dataset containing 137 hospitals and 6,341 prediction tasks demonstrate that the proposed framework achieves CPC = 0.5449, Top-1 = 66.19%, Top-5 = 68.03%, and Top-10 = 74.03%, consistently outperforming conventional deep learning baselines on all Top-K metrics (e.g., Top-5 improves by 22.1% over MLP). Compared with Direct LLM using the same backbone without structured reasoning, the proposed framework improves CPC by 87.5% and Top-1 accuracy by 141%, demonstrating that knowledge-grounded behavioral reasoning, rather than raw LLM capability, is the key to accurate, interpretable, and training-free healthcare OD prediction. These findings highlight the potential of urban intelligence to move healthcare mobility modeling beyond data fitting toward knowledge-driven behavioral reasoning.

## 2. 方法论：核心思想、关键技术细节与流程
- **核心思想**：构建行为城市知识图谱（Behavioral Urban Knowledge Graph），将异构城市信息（人口、医疗资源、交通、天气、历史流动等）组织为九类实体（如Origin、Hospital、Weather、Date等）和十二种语义关系（如SIMILAR_ORIGIN、COMPETES_WITH、SIMILAR_TO等）。通过多智能体（五模块：Retrieval、Analysis、Coordinator、Ranking、Verification）协作推理，模拟居民“感知→认知→决策”的医院选择过程。
- **关键技术细节**：
  - **城市上下文表示**：为每个起点-日期对提取六维场景特征（人口特征、流动模式、天气、可达性、需求、推荐），其中可达性量化公式：\(A = e^{-d/5} \times \max(0.5, 1-0.05\cdot Rain) \times \min(1.2, 1+0.1\cdot RD)\)（d距离，Rain降雨量，RD道路密度）。
  - **行为偏好推理**：Coordinator Agent执行四步链式推理（CoT）：需求识别→可达性评估→能力匹配→综合权衡，输出偏好分数与自然语言推理轨迹。同时维护场景模式记忆（索引为POI主导类型、时间类型、天气等级），实现跨场景的推理一致性。
  - **偏好聚合**：综合LLM推理置信度、历史偏好、医院吸引力、可达性，公式：\(score_h = (\alpha_{conf}\cdot p_h^{LLM} + \beta\cdot hist_h + \gamma\cdot attract_h + \delta\cdot access_h)/(\alpha_{conf}+\beta+\gamma+\delta)\)，默认权重0.4/0.3/0.2/0.1，当推理与历史冲突时LLM动态调整权重。
  - **贝叶斯行为校准**：将推理偏好转化为全局概率分布。历史先验 \(P_{prior}(h|o)\) 由历史流量计算，似然 \(L_h = (p_h^{LLM})^\tau \cdot attract_h^\eta \cdot e^{-d/R_0}\)，后验 \(Posterior_h = P_{prior}(h|o)^w \cdot L_h\)，最后经Softmax归一化。引入三个一致性约束（概率、排序、历史分布），违反则增加历史权重并迭代直至收敛。
- **整体流程**：输入(起点o, 日期t) → 知识图谱检索候选医院 → 场景上下文构建 → 多智能体行为推理 → 偏好聚合 → 贝叶斯校准 → 输出所有医院概率分布。

## 3. 实验设计：数据集、场景与基准方法

- **基准方法（Baselines）**：
